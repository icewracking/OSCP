About this log

01

This is a public record of working through TJNull's OSCP-prep machine list — a mix of HTB and Proving Grounds boxes chosen to mirror OSCP's exam style. Each entry is documented the way a real engagement would be, not just "rooted it," so the writing itself is part of the practice.

It sits alongside a broader certification path: Security+ and CySA+ first, then Pentest+, a PNPT retake, and — once this list is done and the fundamentals are solid — OSCP itself.

Security+→ CySA+→ Pentest+→ PNPT (retake)→ TJNull list→ OSCP

Methodology

02

  • Recon

    Full port scan, service enumeration, and a plain-language note on what's actually exposed before touching anything.

  • Enumeration

    Dig into each service — versions, misconfigurations, exposed shares, default creds — and log every dead end, not just what worked.

  • Foothold

    The exploit or misconfiguration that gets initial access, with the exact commands used and why that path was chosen over others.

  • Privilege escalation

    Path from initial shell to root/system — enumeration first, then the specific technique, mirroring how it'd be explained in a client report.

  • Lessons

    What was slow, what was missed on a first pass, and what pattern is worth remembering for the next box.
